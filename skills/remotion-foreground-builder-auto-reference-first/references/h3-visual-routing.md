# H3 director routing

In this foreground-builder variant, use the active opportunity review in `reference-first-integration.md` for H3 selection. This document supplies visual grammars, not a competing whole-video editorial hierarchy. Necessary deterministic Remotion foreground remains active above an H3 full-screen takeover.

This routing layer adapts the useful planning ideas from the community project [minimax-h3-broll-generator](https://github.com/guangjun5952/minimax-h3-broll-generator) to a talking-head editor. It is not a copy of that project and does not replace the official MiniMax H3 prompt protocol.

## Choose complementary visual functions

Use the established Foreground Scenes and exact spoken anchors; tighten selected shot intervals with the duration director rather than segmenting the whole video again.

- Real interfaces, results, comments, recordings, and sources prove claims through the existing real-object foreground plan. Supplied footage may already provide the concrete experience; assess the actual asset before adding a redundant shot.
- Exact text, numbers, labels, order, comparisons, and diagrams belong to deterministic Remotion foreground. Their presence does not disqualify a complementary H3 scene, comparison, or spatial demonstration.
- `MINIMAX_H3` can embody a concept, show a scenario, role interaction, spatial relationship, contrast, emotional consequence, or causal mechanism when it materially helps understanding, feeling, or recall.
- `MINIMAX_H3_PACKAGING` may animate an environment around a real asset only while the evidence itself remains unchanged and under the foreground plan's ownership.
- Keep A-roll where the presenter's expression, credibility, direct address, or a pause is the stronger treatment.

There is no one-primary-route restriction across H3 and Remotion. Judge the complete composition and preserve meaningful complementary layers. Never use generation to impersonate evidence.

These are semantic grammars, not eligibility gates. The legacy `HYPERFRAMES` label means deterministic foreground here, not an instruction to replace Remotion or forbid H3 on the same scene. Preferred routes describe the named treatment, not exclusive ownership of its subject. A template may be rendered with warm paper collage, editorial cut paper, white diorama, archive desk, low-poly illustration, or another coherent branch. Do not rotate styles randomly within one video.

## H3 shot design

Use the balanced default profile:

- one core idea;
- three or four temporal phases under the existing prompt contract, not mandatory separate causal events;
- only the meaningful elements needed by the scene; four to seven is an optional complexity guide, not a minimum;
- no more than two supporting groups competing at once;
- one camera move followed by a settle;
- one unmistakable action, interaction, contrast, or meaningful reveal; a physical state change is optional;
- a readable end state held long enough to register;
- no generic “tech particles” unless they literally carry meaning.

Keep exact paragraphs and labels in Remotion. If multiple actions overload a shot, simplify or split around a coherent point rather than rejecting it for containing more than one verb. If generated text is essential to truth, do not generate it.

## Asset and truth audit

Before generation, mark each required asset `provided`, `existing`, `licensed`, `generatable`, or `missing`. Real evidence must remain pixel-faithful except for crop, perspective, mask, shadow, or placement. H3 packaging can animate the environment around evidence but cannot rewrite its contents.

## Editorial decision record

Use the compact `analysis/h3-opportunity-review.json` defined in `reference-first-integration.md` for every Foreground Scene. Keep selected shot detail in `analysis/h3-candidates.json`; do not maintain a second routing ledger. Preserve reasons for not selecting or for blocking plausible opportunities, without score thresholds or coverage quotas.
